GistCity — Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10 · effective 15 October 2026 · GistCity is operated by Riel Inc.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, hashed password, username, display name, and your acceptance of the terms, this policy and the age confirmation (with the time of acceptance).
  • Game data: your character's appearance, position in the city while you play, needs, inventory, wallet balance and the ledger of G transactions, achievements and job history.
  • Technical: standard server logs (IP address, browser type) kept for security.

What we do not collect

We never access your camera, microphone, contacts or files without an explicit action from you. The optional face-scan feature (when available) only runs when you start it, and you can always skip it.

Why

To run the game: sign you in, show your character to other players, keep your progress, operate the economy fairly and keep the city safe. We do not sell personal data and we do not show third-party advertising.

Who can see what

Other players see your display name, username, character and public profile. Your email, consents and ledger details are private to you and to the operators of the game.

Where

Data is stored with Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL and authentication). Static game files are served by Cloudflare / GitHub Pages.

Your rights

You can view and change your profile in the game, change your password, and request export or deletion of your account and data at any time via the Account panel or the contact below. Deletion removes your personal data; anonymised economy records may be kept for integrity of the ledger.

Children

GistCity is for people aged 16 and over. We delete accounts of younger users when we become aware of them.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/dyceelvk/Gistcity.
